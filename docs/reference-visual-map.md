## The most important composition correction

The first implementation was a refined **editorial portfolio**: pine background, gold italic words, structured service rows, geometric equipment, metadata, paper cards. Santioni is a **cinematic graphic world**: enormous expressive uppercase title over an immersive ink surface, isolated small brand mark, white fixed mode plaque, bold serif title collisions, landscapes/architecture, deeply filled engraved objects, noise, and abrupt single-color changes. Copying only a hatching texture cannot bridge that difference.

A faithful portfolio translation should have:

1. A full-viewport charcoal opening scene with a huge centered title such as **INTERDIMENSIONAL / TELEVISION**, with intentionally compressed display forms, subtle diagonal title rotation/collisions where legibility survives, and a smaller one-person/many-frequencies line.
2. Full-bleed engraved broadcast landscape or architectural room with solid white masses, black shadow planes, dense hatch shading and the receiver integrated in the environment—not a small outlined icon on a generic gradient.
3. A small upper-left Jahan signature and a white, slightly irregular top-right **ROOM / WORK** plaque, plus a tiny persistent waveform lower-right.
4. A white/light chapter or work scene where a receiver is the large central artifact with bold uppercase project names and short side copy. Keep semantic portfolio navigation instantly available through WORK.
5. A strong red signal accent in a later chapter, or controlled color change when tuning, rather than retaining a single green/gold palette throughout.
6. Native DOM controls and readable work content behind the style. The user asked to copy the visual design; inaccessible canvas-only navigation and alcohol gating do not belong in the portfolio.

## Typography substitution

The fetched font names identify proprietary assets; the repository does not establish a license to distribute those font binaries. Do not silently download and bundle them into the folio. A useful open-source approximation for the display is **Bodoni Moda** at 700–900, uppercase, with controlled horizontal compression and tight line stacking. It provides Didone contrast and narrow serifs closer to the reference's title than Instrument Serif. Charles Rosie has unusual custom swashes and overlapping O forms, so an exact title silhouette would require drawn portfolio lettering or a licensed font; a standard italic headline will not match. Use a bold geometric/rounded grotesque for small uppercase UI and a light neutral grotesque for supporting copy.

## Compatibility-adjusted browser capture

After the user explicitly approved trust configuration, the root agent installed the environment's existing proxy CA in Chromium's trust database. TLS verification remained enabled. The site's own GPUBlocklist refused SwiftShader; the root-run capture therefore fulfilled the cached production app with one compatibility-only edit, removing `swiftshader` from that blocklist. These are **compatibility-modified runtime observations**, not an unmodified supported-device capture. The rendered layout, geometry, palette and title forms are useful visual evidence; software-WebGL text aliasing and post-resize scene positioning must not be treated as original design details.

Root ran `/tmp/santioni-capture/inspect-scenes.mjs`: initial load, age gate YES at the known button position, 6-second wait, wheel1000, wheel3000, then COLLECTION. Each stage wrote a screenshot.

- `compat-intro.png`: huge custom white uppercase title centered across approximately 70% of a charcoal viewport. The title has compressed/tall letterforms, exaggerated overlapping O shapes, and an oversized final E/C swash. Dark flowing engraved brush strata fill the background. An irregular white cloud/tear rises from the bottom. Small logo upper-left; white rough-edged mode plaque upper-right; white waveform lower-right. This establishes a much closer first-screen composition than the previous split marketing hero.

The main panel contains charcoal sky and white cloud masses. A tan cloaked figure is filled with black shadow and dense directional line engraving. A second overlapping close-up comic panel occupies the lower-left. This confirms a graphic-novel panel structure, not just a hatch shader.
- `compat-scroll-2.png`: a vast cream staircase leads toward a cream monolith with a red circular portal. A large tan-cloaked figure sits in the foreground. Dense black contour and hatch lines describe the surfaces. A black-outlined narrator caption appears upper-left. Most of the image is warm white paper, with charcoal sky and small tan/red tones.
- `compat-collection.png`: full vermilion-red viewport. An enormous stacked white display product name occupies the center. A tall tilted glass bottle is layered in front of and occludes the title. Small flavor labels sit at left/right midheight; black bold product statistics sit at bottom center. The fixed white mode plaque and upper-left logo persist. This is the clearest work-view composition to translate into the portfolio: giant project title behind a large tilted receiver artifact, sparse side metadata, strong single-color field.
- `compat-mobile-collection.png` landed in a lower page/footer after resizing the already-scrolled desktop page. It does **not** establish the mobile collection scene layout. Do not use that screenshot as proof of an intended collection composition.

The screenshots support charcoal title → white graphic-novel panels → vivid red object-and-type collection as the overall visual rhythm. A portfolio can copy that rhythm and composition while using its own title, receiver, working material and semantic controls.
