# The complete network — project presentation bible

This is a creative specification, not a record of completed client work. All nineteen working treatments are available at `/work/<slug>/treatment`; unreleased `/work/<slug>` routes also open their clearly marked shell. The original five readable entries retain their current provenance.

## Shared identity and interaction contract

Primary visual reference: [Santioni Spirits](https://santionispirits.com/). Reconstruct the charcoal monumental title, white comic framing, controlled engraving, and red collection stage; retain original portfolio artwork and project metaphors. Source and compatibility-adjusted observations are in reference-study.md. Other links are proposed design precedents, not newly audited runtime evidence.

- Title: Bodoni Moda uppercase, tight display spacing; body: Newsreader on paper; controls: DM Sans. The signature is Instrument Serif.
- Palette: ink #1d1d1d, paper #f5f3e9, vermilion #c82924. Project middle tones are supplied in treatments.json. All essential text needs contrast verification against its final surface.
- Header: stable white Experience/Archive plaque, red ornament, small broadcaster signature, persistent contact and Index. Native focus remains visible; Escape closes the menu and dialogs.
- Scroll: native document scroll and real hash anchors; chapter progress via IntersectionObserver. Sticky navigation must leave room for focused elements. No scroll-lock journey or compulsory drag.
- Timing: feedback80–150ms; spatial450–600ms cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1); atmosphere8 steps/sec at most. The design guide shows timing samples. Reading remains still; reduced motion removes atmospheric movement.
- Project transition: title, selected channel, accent, destination and provenance must update together. A visual treatment is not labelled verified work.
- Images: preserve aspect ratio, supply descriptive alt text and captions, and retain text navigation on failure. No essential captions exclusively inside canvas.
- Contact: prefill only the relevant frequency. Native validation; browser-tab draft; explicit email-composer delivery model. No fake sent state.
- Responsive: large object and title on desktop; readable labels beneath on mobile. Controls and reading always work without hover or a GPU.
- Evidence: source text, role, permission-cleared artifacts, revisions, actual outcomes, credits. Empty slots carry an explicit placeholder label; metrics are optional.

### CH. 000 — A borough becomes a constellation

**Story kernel:** From isolated voices to shared civic imagination.
**Identity:** `constellation` visual family; middle-tone accent `#aebf9f`; shared ink, paper, monumental type and comic borders.

**Three story scenes:** A map with no centre → Connect the voices → A shared horizon.
**Motion concept:** Isolated nodes → connected relationships → stable map.
**Interaction / exact-work presentation:** Hover/focus highlights a source; click opens its citation. Never fabricate quantitative network relationships.
**Content to supply:** Anonymous location pins; participation map; research synthesis; role/source disclosure.
**Reference:** [The Pudding](https://pudding.cool/) — proposed editorial or interaction precedent.

**Prototype now:** three rendered scenes; variable SVG motif; keyboard-operable structure control; current chapter indicator; director notes; six evidence slots; reference desk; next-channel link and relevant contact action.

## Editing without breaking the world

1. Edit `src/data/treatments.json` for story direction, motif, three chapters, accent, asset requirements and reference.
2. Keep source/provenance and exact writing in `src/data/transmissions.ts`; treatment concepts do not automatically promote archive status.
3. Replace an illustrative artifact only after verifying its authorship, date and publication permission. Add text alternatives.
4. Confirm real titles, exact role, collaborators and objective before publishing as a case study.
5. Validate keyboard behavior, reduced motion, image failure, viewport fit, reading measure, deep links and contact composition.
6. Treat page-specific animation sketches as proposals until implemented and tested with actual assets. Current working controls demonstrate structure, not final historical content.
